# RajAudios / Rajify logo

Generated using the built-in imagegen tool. The approved symbol combines sound waves with a play silhouette in the app's violet palette.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Create one exceptional SYMBOL-ONLY app icon for RajAudios / Rajify, a premium music discovery and listening app. No text, no letters, no initials. Design a memorable custom emblem combining a PLAY triangle with a flowing AUDIO WAVE: three thick rounded rising sound ribbons sweep together into a forward-pointing play silhouette, with a clean triangular negative-space opening. A single beautifully balanced compact symbol, expressive rhythm, clever negative space, professionally crafted geometry. Use luminous lavender #c4b5fd and rich violet #8b5cf6 with a restrained violet-to-lavender gradient on the symbol; solid almost-black ink #09090f full-bleed opaque square background. The mark occupies 72% of the square with equal generous margins. Bold forms, minimal detail, clear at 16 and 32 pixels. Sophisticated music software identity. Flat vector-like graphic, crisp edges. No music-note clip art, no headphones, no equalizer in a circle, no lightning bolt, no star, no photo, no mockup, no extra borders, no drop shadow, no watermark. One final icon only.

## Final refinement prompt

Refine this exact RajAudios sound-wave/play symbol for production favicon use. Preserve the three flowing violet/lavender ribbons, triangular negative space, scale and composition. Remove all stray blue, magenta, white pixels and irregular halos around the silhouette. Edges must be perfectly clean antialiased. Put the symbol on a completely solid opaque dark charcoal #09090f background, including every gap and the triangle opening; no transparency anywhere. Keep the gentle violet to lavender gradient INSIDE the symbol only. No glow, no texture, no grain, no added objects, no text. Single clean square app icon.
